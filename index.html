    Do I need my FPGEC certificate before the NAPLEX?

    If you studied pharmacy outside the US, yes. You complete FPGEC certification, which includes the FPGEE and an English proficiency requirement, before you can sit the NAPLEX. Intern hour rules vary by state.

    Do you cover the TOEFL?

    We cover the TOEFL iBT as part of the FPGEC journey: the section minimums, the new MyBest score rules, and how to plan your test around your FPGEE date.

    Can I become a pharmacy technician without a degree?

    Yes. To sit the PTCB’s PTCE you complete a PTCB-recognised training programme or 500 hours of pharmacy technician work. State rules for technician registration also apply.

    Do you share real exam questions?

    No. Sharing recalled NAPLEX, FPGEE or PTCE questions breaks the testing agencies’ rules. All practice material is original and exam-style.
